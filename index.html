Welcome to League Night

Runs your club's darts, shuffleboard, pool and cornhole leagues from your phone: the players or teams, a season of fixtures, the table, a live scorer, and knock-out tournaments. One club, one roster, as many leagues as you play. Anyone signed in to Google with the club's ID can follow it; anyone with its admin key can run it.

Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and share a club. Until then it lives in this browser only.

Create a Club asks for the club's name and the first league inside it — its game, and whether it is played as singles or in teams — then opens on the Players tab. Add the people once, start a season, and the fixtures generate themselves. More leagues, at any game, go on the Club tab afterwards.

Sign In brings back the clubs already on your Google account — this browser opens on the one you were in last, and you need no Club ID for a club you have already joined.

Join a Club asks for the Club ID someone sent you — and the Admin Key, if they want you running it — and signs you in to Google, because a shared club is tied to real accounts.

Try the Demo loads five invented clubs. The first runs two leagues at once on one roster — a darts league with a season under way, handicaps, a finished knockout and a double-elimination cup in progress, and a shuffleboard league scored frame by frame, with two players carrying a handicap at each game. Then a doubles darts club with team matches; a shuffleboard club; a pool club scored rack by rack, with two players on the wire; and a cornhole club of four pairs, with a lineup card, a sub and games scored round by round — so every tab, and all four games, have something on them.

Back Up & Restore

One JSON file holding everything in this club — a copy you keep, independent of this browser and of the cloud.

Every match as a spreadsheet. It is a one-way door: the CSV is for reading elsewhere, and Restore reads the JSON above, never this.

Restoring a backup of a league already on this device replaces that league; a backup of another league is added beside the ones you have. You are told what the file holds and asked to confirm first.

Start Again

Deletes every league on this device. A league you have shared stays in the cloud for its members and comes back when you sign in; a league that lives only here is gone for good. Your settings and your theme are kept. To delete one league, or to delete a shared league for everyone, use the Club tab.

Share a Read-Only Link

Anyone opening this link sees the league as it is right now, with no way to change it and no sign-in. Everything travels inside the link itself — nothing is uploaded, and it carries no Club ID, so it is a picture of the club and not a way in.

How Much

What to Include

Your Link
