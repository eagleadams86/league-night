Privacy Policy

Effective 1 January 2026

Everything you type stays in this browser. There is no account, no server, and nothing is uploaded.

What Is Stored

Your items, your choice of colour theme and which tab you last had open, saved in this browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

What Is Not

  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • No third-party scripts of any kind — the page loads nothing beyond its own files.
  • No personal information is asked for or kept.

Backups

A backup is a plain JSON file written to wherever your browser puts downloads. It never leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

Changes

If what the app stores ever changes, this page and its effective date are updated in the same commit as the change.
